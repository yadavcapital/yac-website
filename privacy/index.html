Privacy policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

In short

  • You can use YAC without an account. If you sign in with Google, we keep your name, email and an account ID.
  • Your portfolio, plans, alerts and scanned documents stay on your phone.
  • We don't sell your data and we don't use analytics tracking.
  • There are no ads in YAC. Payments go through Razorpay, and we never see your card or UPI details.

1. Who we are

YAC ("we", "us") is a personal-finance education app published by Yadav Capital ([proprietor full legal name]), [Full postal address, city, state, PIN]. The app ID is in.yadavcapital.yac. For anything about your data, write to yadavcapital@yahoo.com.

2. What we collect and why

Account (optional)

If you choose "Continue with Google", Google and our sign-in provider, Firebase Authentication (Google LLC), give us your name, email address, profile photo and a unique account ID. We use these only to sign you in and to reply if you contact us. If you choose "Continue without an account", none of this is collected.

What you enter in the app

Holdings, quantities, prices, goals, plans, your loss limit and alert settings are stored on your device. We do not upload them to our own servers.

Documents and camera scans

When you scan or open a financial document, YAC reads it on your phone using on-device text recognition to find risks and charges. The pictures and text are not sent to YAC. Camera photos are kept in a temporary folder on your phone and deleted automatically within a day.

Market data, news and logos

To show prices, charts, company figures and news, the app requests public data for the symbols and names you look at from providers such as Yahoo Finance, Google News, Alpha Vantage and Eulerpool. Company and platform logos are fetched from public icon services (for example Google, DuckDuckGo and Parqet) using the company's ticker or website name. These requests contain the symbol or search term, not your portfolio or identity. Like any website, these providers can see your device's IP address.

Saved summaries

A PDF summary of a document check is saved only if you choose to save it, to the place you pick.

Emails you send us

Sponsor enquiries open your own email app. We receive only what you choose to send.

No ads

YAC does not show ads and does not include any advertising SDK. We do not collect or use your advertising ID.

Support payments

Voluntary support payments are processed by Razorpay Software Pvt. Ltd. Razorpay collects the payment details you enter. We receive only the payment ID, amount, status and the contact details you gave Razorpay. We never see or store full card numbers, UPI PINs or bank passwords. See Razorpay's privacy policy.

Alerts

Alerts are checked on your device in the background, about every few hours while you are online. Nothing about your portfolio is sent to us for this.

3. What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent or trade your personal data.
  • We do not use analytics or tracking tools inside the app.
  • We do not read your SMS, contacts, call logs or location.
  • We do not place trades or move money for you.

4. Phone permissions

  • Camera: only when you scan a document.
  • Notifications: to show your alerts.
  • Internet: to fetch prices, news and sign-in.

5. Backups

If Android backup is on, your phone may back up YAC's portfolio and settings to your own Google account. Your sign-in details are excluded from these backups.

6. Links to other apps

The Invest section links to brokers such as Zerodha and Vested. Some links are affiliate links, which means we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Once you leave YAC, that company's privacy policy applies.

7. How long we keep data

Your account details are kept while your account exists. On-device data stays until you delete it in the app, clear the app's storage or uninstall. Payment records are kept for as long as Indian tax and accounting law requires.

8. Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask to see, correct or delete your personal data, withdraw consent, and raise a grievance. To delete your account, follow the steps on our delete account page. For anything else, email yadavcapital@yahoo.com. We reply within 7 days and resolve requests within 30 days.

Grievance officer: Yadav Capital ([proprietor full legal name]), yadavcapital@yahoo.com, [Full postal address, city, state, PIN].

9. Security

All connections from the app use HTTPS. Sign-in is handled by Google. No method is perfectly secure, but we keep what we hold to a minimum.

10. Children

YAC is meant for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has signed in, email us and we will delete the account.

11. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above. For important changes, we will also tell you in the app.
