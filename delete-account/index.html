Delete your YAC account

Last updated: 30 October 2026

This page explains how to delete your YAC account and data.

Delete your account

  • Email yadavcapital@yahoo.com from the Google email you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my YAC account".
  • We delete your account (name, email, profile photo and account ID) within 30 days and confirm by email.

Delete data on your phone

  • Use Remove all in Portfolio, or clear YAC's storage in Android Settings → Apps → YAC → Storage, or uninstall the app.
  • If Android backup was on, delete the backup from your Google account (Google Drive → Backups).

What we may keep

Records of support payments (payment ID, amount, date) are kept for up to 8 years, because Indian tax and accounting law requires it. Nothing else is kept.
