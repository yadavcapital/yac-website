Cancellation & refunds

Last updated: 31 October 2026

In short: support payments are voluntary, so they are not refunded. Monthly support can be cancelled at any time and stops future charges. If you were charged twice or a payment failed but money left your account, we will return it.

1. Support payments are voluntary

YAC is free. Payments made through the "Support YAC" screen, whether one-time or monthly, are voluntary contributions. They do not unlock any product, service or feature. For this reason, we do not offer refunds on completed support payments, including if you change your mind.

2. When we do return money

  • Duplicate charge: you were charged more than once for the same payment.
  • Failed payment: money left your account but the payment shows as failed, or we never received it.
  • Wrong amount: you were charged a different amount from the one shown, because of a technical error.
  • Unauthorised payment: a payment you did not make, once confirmed with Razorpay, Google Play or your bank.

3. How to ask

Email yadavcapital@yahoo.com within 30 days of the payment. Include the date, amount and the Razorpay payment ID, Google Play order number or bank reference. We reply within 2 working days.

Approved returns go back to the original payment method within 5–7 working days of approval. Your bank may take a few extra days to show it. Failed payments are usually reversed automatically by your bank or Razorpay within the same period.

4. Cancellation

One-time payments cannot be cancelled once completed.

Monthly support is billed through Google Play. You can cancel it at any time in the Play Store app under Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. After you cancel, you will not be charged again; months already paid are not refunded. Payments made through Google Play are also covered by Google Play's own refund policy.
