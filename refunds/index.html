Cancellation & refunds

Last updated: 30 October 2026

In short: support payments are voluntary, so they are not refunded. If you were charged twice or a payment failed but money left your account, we will return it.

1. Support payments are voluntary

YAC is free. Payments made through the "Support YAC" screen are one-time, voluntary contributions. They do not buy any product, service, subscription or feature. For this reason, we do not offer refunds on completed support payments, including if you change your mind.

2. When we do return money

  • Duplicate charge: you were charged more than once for the same payment.
  • Failed payment: money left your account but the payment shows as failed, or we never received it.
  • Wrong amount: you were charged a different amount from the one shown, because of a technical error.
  • Unauthorised payment: a payment you did not make, once confirmed with Razorpay or your bank.

3. How to ask

Email yadavcapital@yahoo.com within 30 days of the payment. Include the date, amount and the Razorpay payment ID or bank reference. We reply within 2 working days.

Approved returns go back to the original payment method within 5–7 working days of approval. Your bank may take a few extra days to show it. Failed payments are usually reversed automatically by your bank or Razorpay within the same period.

4. Cancellation

There are no subscriptions or recurring payments in YAC, so there is nothing to cancel. Once a payment is completed, it cannot be cancelled.
